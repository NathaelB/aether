A data plane only becomes selectable for new deployments once its first
heartbeat lands. Until then the control plane reports it as never seen, and
placement skips it.
On a fresh install, Herald and Genesis restart a few times before settling:
neither retries its first connection to the broker, so both exit while RabbitMQ
is still starting and Kubernetes restarts them until it answers. Expect a
minute of CrashLoopBackOff on a cold cluster; it is not a broken install.

restarts. Set rabbitmq.externalUrl for anything beyond a local cluster.
